# Contact and Relationship Tracking

## Purpose
Give the user a simple place to remember important people and professional context.

## Expected behavior
The user can create, view, edit, and remove contacts with basic identity and professional details, relationship context, and optional links to tasks, commitments, notes, and meetings.

## Inputs / outputs
The user supplies contact details and relationship information. The app stores the record and displays its details and linked activity.

## User-visible behavior
Contact lists and detail views make important people easy to find and show related responsibilities and notes.

## Constraints
This is lightweight relationship tracking for one professional, not a shared CRM or external address-book sync.

## Basic acceptance expectations
Contact records can be managed, searched, and linked to other records without losing relationship context.
