A simple Executive Assistant Web App for an individual professional. Use Bootstrap
for the frontend and FastAPI+SQLite for the backend.

The app helps the user organize daily responsibilities, track priorities,
prepare for upcoming meetings, remember follow-ups, and keep important notes and
contacts in one place.

Start with a small set of tasks, upcoming commitments, contacts, notes, and
reminders as seed data.

Major capabilities:

1. Daily dashboard with priorities, upcoming commitments, overdue items, and
   quick actions.
2. Task management with priorities, due dates, completion, postponement, and
   recurring tasks.
3. Schedule and commitment tracking for meetings and other important events.
4. Meeting preparation and follow-up notes.
5. Contact and relationship tracking for important people.
6. Quick capture inbox for tasks, notes, and reminders that need processing.
7. Search across the assistant's stored information.
8. Basic reminders for upcoming and overdue responsibilities.
